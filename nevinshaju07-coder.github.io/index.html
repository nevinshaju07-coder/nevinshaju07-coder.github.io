01 // about

          
Securing AI systems, from the SOC side.

          
I secure Generative AI and agentic AI systems, and I come at it from the security operations side. After a year on a 24/7 MDR team investigating real incidents in Microsoft Sentinel and Splunk, I started breaking and defending LLM applications: testing prompt injection and jailbreaks, building guardrails, and writing detections mapped to MITRE ATLAS and the OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications. I have built my own agentic SOC and I am building Haris AI, an AI security gateway for UAE organisations. I hold an MSc in Cyber Security and rank in the top 1% on TryHackMe.
